Scope, assumptions and exclusions

  • Rates for FY 2026-27 (AY 2027-28) under the Income-tax Act, 2025 / Finance Act, 2026. Slabs are unchanged from FY 2025-26.
  • Assumes a resident individual below 60 years. Age groups 60+ are not supported; their old-regime slabs differ.
  • New regime: standard deduction ₹75,000 on salary; section 87A rebate up to ₹60,000 when taxable income is ₹12,00,000 or less, with marginal relief just above that limit.
  • Old regime: standard deduction ₹50,000; section 87A rebate up to ₹12,500 when taxable income is ₹5,00,000 or less. Above that, no rebate and no marginal relief applies (verified against the official Income Tax Department calculator, which computes full slab tax just above ₹5,00,000). Deductions you enter are applied in full.
  • Surcharge: 10% above ₹50L, 15% above ₹1Cr, 25% above ₹2Cr (new regime cap); old regime 37% above ₹5Cr. Surcharge marginal relief is applied. Health and Education Cess 4% added.
  • Excluded: capital gains and other special-rate income (sec 111A/112/112A), HRA and other exemptions, professional tax, family pension rules, clubbing and set-off of losses, and the alternate regime for business income opt-outs.
  • This is an estimate, not tax advice. Verify on the Income Tax Department site before filing.
  • Sources: incometaxindia.gov.in tax rates, Finance Bill 2026 memorandum (indiabudget.gov.in).
